Frequently asked questions

Is Nearby really free?

Yes. Creating a business profile, responding to requests, hosting gatherings, and running offers are all free to start — there's no credit card required and no self-serve paid plan today.

Do I have to offer a discount to participate?

No. You can participate through gatherings, signature experiences, availability postings, or simply responding to a real request with your usual terms — a discount is one option among several, never a requirement.

Do I have to constantly post to stay visible?

No. Tell Nearby what your business is great for once, and update it whenever something real changes — you're not expected to run a content calendar.

How does matching actually work?

Nearby looks at more than distance — your categories, cuisine and attributes, timing, weather, and how reliably you've followed through on past requests all factor in, so a farther but better-fitting business can genuinely rank above a closer one.

Is my business content reviewed?

Yes. Real business-authored content is checked before certain changes go live, with a periodic re-check afterward too — to help keep what's on the platform safe and legitimate.

Do I need an existing online presence?

No. If we can find your business through search, we'll pre-fill what we can. If not, you can add your business manually. Finding your business or adding it manually takes about 30 seconds. After you submit, a real person reviews the application before it goes live.

What happens after I submit my application?

A real person reviews every application before it goes live. We'll let you know as soon as it's approved.

Can I edit my profile after I'm approved?

Yes — your name, description, logo, category, attributes, and address are all editable any time from your dashboard.
