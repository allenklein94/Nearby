Frequently asked questions

Is Nearby really free?

Yes. Creating a business profile, hosting gatherings, and running offers are all free to start — there's no credit card required and no self-serve paid plan today.

Do I need an existing online presence?

No. If we can find your business through search, we'll pre-fill what we can. If not, you can add your business manually — either way takes about 30 seconds.

What happens after I submit my application?

A real person reviews every application before it goes live. We'll let you know as soon as it's approved.

Can I edit my profile after I'm approved?

Yes — your name, description, logo, category, and address are all editable any time from your dashboard.

What can I actually do once I'm approved?

Host gatherings and communities, create and run offers, message your followers, and see real analytics — including how people are discovering your profile.
