# Nearby sound cues

`offer-arrived.wav`, `offer-accepted.wav` and `visit-confirmed.wav` are original works made for Nearby (owner idea "169",
2026-10-18). They are synthesized entirely from sine tones by `scripts/sounds/generate-sound-cues.py`; no samples, recordings,
sound libraries or third-party material were used, so no outside licence applies. Nearby owns them like the rest of its source.
Regenerate them with that script; never replace them with downloaded sounds without recording that sound's licence here.
