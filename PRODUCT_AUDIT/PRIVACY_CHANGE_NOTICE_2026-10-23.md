**Subject:** Updates to Nearby's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Hi,

We've updated Nearby's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (effective October 23, 2026). Here is what changed:

**Google Maps.** Some Nearby features (place search, address search, maps for choosing a location, and directions) are
provided by Google Maps Platform. The Terms now say that using them is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. The
Privacy Policy now explains what is sent to Google when you use them: your device's location for a place search, the words of
that search, and any address you type on a map. We never send Google your name, email address, profile or messages.

**Gathering locations.** When you host a Gathering, we store the meeting point you choose. People browsing see only an
approximate distance and, if you show the Gathering on the map, an approximate pin. The exact meeting point is shown only to
you and to the people you approve to attend.

**Crossed Paths.** The record that you crossed paths with someone no longer includes either person's actual location, only an
approximate area. It now expires 48 hours after the last time you were near each other. After that it is no longer shown and
is deleted automatically. If you cross paths again later, a new record is created, and you may get a new "You crossed paths!"
notification. Matches, Notices and messages are not affected. The rounded location used to detect crossings is still deleted
after one hour.

You can read the full documents here:
- Terms of Service: https://allenklein94.github.io/Nearby/terms.html
- Privacy Policy: https://allenklein94.github.io/Nearby/privacy.html

If you keep using Nearby after these changes take effect, you accept the updated documents. If you have questions, reply to
this email or contact us at the address in the Privacy Policy.

The Nearby team
